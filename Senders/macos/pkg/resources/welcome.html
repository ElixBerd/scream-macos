Scream for macOS

Scream sends your Mac's sound to Scream receivers on your local network (Linux, Windows, ESP32 and others) with very low delay.

This installs:

  • Scream Audio, an output device you can pick in the Sound menu
  • Scream, a menu bar app to turn streaming on and off, choose the receiver, latency and quality
  • screamctl, the same settings from Terminal

Sound stops for a second while Core Audio reloads. After installing, Scream starts in the menu bar, switches your output to Scream Audio and opens at every login. Turn either off from its menu.
